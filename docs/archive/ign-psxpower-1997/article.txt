PSXPower Yaroze Contest Winner
The winner of the Yaroze Contest announced, plus their winning essay
BY IGN STAFF
UPDATED: JUN 21, 2012 1:38PM PDT
0 COMMENTS
Add IGN on Google
Many would-be programmers entered our contest to win a Yaroze development kit, but unfortunately only one could win. Tim Otholt's entry impressed us with its vision, realism, and brevity. Congratulations Tim.

Below is Tim Otholt's essay in full:

I will change the face of gaming by introducing new technologies, and by showing the PSX pushed to the limit. Although not revolutionary, my goals are realistic and very attainable with just myself. Such ideas include:

1. Streaming live video texture maps from the CD onto 3D polygon characters. This makes possible the Babylon 5 effect and although it is capable, it is underutilized on the PSX.

2. Inventing a new input device, which I can't disclose, but will hopefully push 3D fighting games into a whole new realm.

3. Improve on the 3D gaming model, including new camera motion algorithms for more interesting viewpoints.

4. Show that good AI can be implemented on any gaming system, given that you are ready to model how real people play, and that you can save this information for replay later. My incremental advancements are more advanced versions of the LEARN mode in Saturn VF2.

I know my essay is not earth shattering. Earth shattering is probably what you want to hear, but I know for a fact that earth shattering can be done on any platform, not just the Yaroze. I want to show that the little guy can do it, and you don't have to be Namco or Square to do it right.

Thanks to everyone who entered.
